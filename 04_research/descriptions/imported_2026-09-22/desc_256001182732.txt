Designed as an ideal LED replacement for existing SON or HQI. It is a super bright and high-powered light, while also being highly efficient, which in turn increases the lifespan of the bulb. This overall means reduced maintenance costs as well as great energy-saving benefits. With a built-in driver and available in some of the most common socket bases, it is suitable for countless applications.

Specification

• Base: B22/E14/E27

• Wattage: 12W/16W/20W

• Input Voltage: AC85-265V

• Colour Temperature: 3000K/4000K/6400K

• Beam Angle: 360°

• CRI: >80

• Warranty: 2 Years
