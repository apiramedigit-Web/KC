How it was done (Keyword Analysis.pdf)

  • Keywords = phrases repeated across that Product ID’s final competitor titles (short-tail 1–2 words, long-tail 3+). Competitor Keywords = repeated competitor phrases missing from our own title.
  • Last 30 Days Sales: NOT VERIFIED. eBay’s purchase-history page needs sign-in behind a CAPTCHA, which is never bypassed.
