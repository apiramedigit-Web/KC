 
35cm Tiered Ceiling Pendant Light Shade

These stylish ceiling pendant shades come in a 3 tone fabric finish and are available in grey, pink and cream finish. They are a great way of bringing a contemporary edge to any room of your home. They simply fit to your existing ceiling light socket - no wiring required. 

Our shades come with a 4cm opening for E27 lamp holders, and we’ve added a handy reducer ring that brings it down to 3cm so it’s E14 friendly too.


Why not add one of our ValueLights branded 6W LED GLS bulbs to your purchase? These energy saving bulbs give equivalent light output to 60W halogen bulbs while using a fraction of the enegry!
Please Note: This item is shade only – electrical fitting is not included.
