

DESCRIPTION

Meet Sophia, the stylish solution you’ve been looking for in a ceiling shade and an exciting take on a classic fabric shade design. With a cylindrical shade at the core, and two stacked shades on the outer, this pendant shade adds style to any room. Whether you’re revamping your living room, bedroom, or office, Sophia will give your home décor a glow up!  Available in 8 colours, grey, cream, black, red, navy, olive green, sand and blush pink, you can find a shade to match your colour scheme.
